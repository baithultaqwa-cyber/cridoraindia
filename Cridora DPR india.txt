Cridora
Final Updated DPR (Version 4.0 — Strategic & Legally Structured)
1. Executive Summary
Cridora is an India-first interoperable precious metals savings, redemption, settlement, and jeweller infrastructure platform.
The platform initially launches with:
•	BIS 916 Gold only 
but is architected to later support:
•	additional gold purities 
•	silver 
•	platinum 
•	other precious metals 
•	commodity-backed metal ecosystems 
through a modular multi-metal infrastructure.
Cridora connects:
•	customers 
•	local jewellers 
•	jewellery chains 
•	savings schemes 
•	redemption systems 
•	liquidity services 
•	transfer infrastructure 
•	settlement networks 
into one unified digital ecosystem.

5.6 Cross-Jeweller Redemption
Users may redeem through different participating jewellers.
Settlement Flow
1.	User requests redemption 
2.	Cridora routes request 
3.	Original jeweller liability identified 
4.	Settlement amount transferred to Cridora 
5.	Cridora settles redeeming jeweller 
6.	Redeeming jeweller fulfills order 
Since the platform tracks:
metal in grams rather than fixed money value,
cross-redemption fees may apply.
User pays:
•	making charges 
•	cross-network settlement fees 
•	jeweller-specific redemption fees 
________________________________________
5.7 Immediate vs Standard Redemption
Immediate Redemption
•	Same jeweller 
•	Instant/Near-instant 
•	Based on local inventory 
Standard Redemption
•	Cross-network 
•	Routed via Cridora settlement engine 
•	Standard fulfillment SLA (example: under 48 hours)

Cridora may independently offer emergency liquidity.

User Receives	₹58,200

a platform-level liquidity service.

GST
Handled by jewellers.
Applicable:
•	3% GST on gold 
•	GST on making charges

future redemption avoids GST recalculation on new metal prices.

User Taxation
Cash sellbacks may trigger:
•	capital gains taxation 
•	depending on Indian tax law and holding period 
Users remain responsible for declarations.
